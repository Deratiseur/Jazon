Jazon is a revenant, a level 8 undead warrior who is very strong and resistant, and once you summon him he follows you around like a familiar.
If he dies, the main character automatically retrieves his heart so that he can summon him again after a rest.
Jazon cannot progress in xp, he will be level 8 forever. But when he kills, he gains +1 to hit and damage for 8H (cumulative)
There is a 5% chance each time you summon Jazon that he will turn hostile.

To find him, search the tombs. In the Atkatla cemetery in BG2, in the Valley of Shadows in IWDEE. You need to start a new game to find his heart.

Jazon starts with a non-magical weapon and gains a magical weapon with the main character's evolution, the enchantment of his weapon being equal to the main character's M xp (example: he is equipped with a +4 weapon when the main character has 4M+ xp) max +5 at 5M xp.
